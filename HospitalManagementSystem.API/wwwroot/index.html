Our Services

Everything you need to manage your care, in one system.

Appointment Booking

Request appointments with your doctor and track their status from request to confirmation.

AI Symptom Assistant

A trained machine learning model helps doctors get a quick, data-driven read on reported symptoms.

Digital Medical Records

Upload and access medical history documents securely, summarized into an easy-to-read timeline.

Vitals Tracking

Doctors log vital signs over time, visualized as charts so trends are easy to spot at a glance.

Doctor-Patient Matching

Patients are assigned to doctors who can review their history and accept or decline new cases.

Email Notifications

Doctors and patients are notified by email when an appointment is requested, confirmed, or denied.

About MediCare HMS

MediCare HMS is a hospital management platform built to streamline the day-to-day work of connecting patients and doctors — from booking and confirming appointments, to keeping medical histories organized, to giving doctors a quick, AI-assisted starting point when reviewing symptoms.
